# NexusOS Demo Video Script (3 Minutes)

## Goal
Showcase the complete user journey from a cold landing page to a deployed, autonomous agent saving tangible business labor cost.

---

### Segment 1: The Promise (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visual:** Screen recording starts on the dark-mode landing page `nexusos.app`.
**Action:** slowly scroll down the hero section.
**VO:** *"Your business runs on human labor. Repetitive, manual tasks across Slack, Jira, and Gmail are silently draining your margins. NexusOS changes that."*
**Action:** Click the "See Demo" button. Let the loader spin for 1 second.
**VO:** *"We built an audit platform that maps your organizational patterns, calculates exactly what they cost, and instantly provisions AI agents to automate them."*

### Segment 2: The Audit (0:30 - 1:15)
**Visual:** Page redirects straight into the NexusOS War Room (Demo Data loaded).
**Action:** Hover over the Executive Summary (Bleed Map, Total Savings).
**VO:** *"Behind the scenes, we just connected to Slack, Gmail, and GitHub. Our pipeline analyzed thousands of events to find workflows. Immediately, we see our biggest leak: Triage requests crossing between Slack and Linear."*
**Action:** Click the `View` button on the top opportunity in the Scored Table.
**VO:** *"Clicking into the workflow, NexusOS has automatically mapped the steps, scored the feasibility at 88%, and calculated the ROI. This single process costs the team $45,000 a year in manual labor."*

### Segment 3: The Deployment (1:15 - 2:00)
**Visual:** Inside the Workflow Details component.
**Action:** Emphasize the **"Deploy Agent"** button. Click it.
**VO:** *"Finding the leak is only half the battle. Now, we plug it."*
**Action:** Navigate to the `/runs` Agent execution page.
**VO:** *"With one click, NexusOS provisions an autonomous worker, tailored precisely to this workflow. It writes its own step configuration based on our audit graph, including read-access to Gmail and write-access to Jira."*

### Segment 4: Agent Collaboration (2:00 - 2:40)
**Visual:** Navigate to the Agent Chains UI.
**Action:** Show the real-time execution logs of an active chain.
**VO:** *"And these agents don't work in isolation. In our War Room, you can chain them together. Here, a Triage Agent evaluates an incoming Slack request, passes the structured data to a Research Agent, and finally hands it to a Resolution Agent to update the GitHub issue."*

### Segment 5: The War Room / Outro (2:40 - 3:00)
**Visual:** Return to the War Room dashboard.
**Action:** Point to the top right 'Live Tracking' stats.
**VO:** *"NexusOS tracks the outcome metrics of every agent execution, updating your total net savings in real-time. Stop guessing where your time goes. Start automating it today with a free audit at nexusos.app."*
